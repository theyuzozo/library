# 🍄 Medicinal Mushroom 药用蘑菇

A quick reference to six medicinal mushrooms: their nature (性质), benefits, main compounds, recommended extraction, and who they suit (or don't).

## At a glance 一览

| Mushroom 蘑菇 | Nature 性质 | Extraction 推荐提取 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[#Chaga 桦褐孔菌]] | Neutral 中性 | Hot water + ethanol 热水＋乙醇 |
| [[#Birch Polypore 桦树多孔菌]] | Neutral 中性 | Hot water + ethanol 热水＋乙醇 |
| [[#Red Belt Conk 红带菌]] | Neutral 中性 | Hot water + ethanol 热水＋乙醇 |
| [[#Artist Conk 画家灵芝]] | Neutral 中性 | Hot water + ethanol 热水＋乙醇 |
| [[#Turkey Tail 云芝]] | Neutral 中性 | Hot water 热水 |
| [[#Reishi / Lingzhi 灵芝]] | Neutral / slightly warm 中性偏温 | Hot water + ethanol 热水＋乙醇 |

> [!tip] Why two extractions? 为什么要双提取？
> Hot water pulls out the water-soluble **polysaccharides** and **β-glucans**; ethanol pulls out the less water-soluble **triterpenoids** and some phenolics. Most of these mushrooms use both (a "dual extract"). Turkey Tail is the exception: its key compounds (PSK, PSP) are polysaccharides, so hot water alone is the main choice.
> 热水主要提取多糖、β-葡聚糖；乙醇主要提取三萜及部分酚类等较疏水成分。云芝的主要成分为多糖类，因此以热水提取为主。

## Chaga 桦褐孔菌

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral 中性
- **Benefits 功效:** Supports antioxidant and immune-related functions; traditionally used for general and digestive wellness.
  抗氧化、免疫相关支持，传统上用于整体体质及消化调理。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** Polysaccharides, triterpenoids, polyphenols, melanin
  多糖、三萜、多酚、黑色素
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water + ethanol. Hot water favors polysaccharides; ethanol favors triterpenoids and some phenolics.
  热水＋乙醇。热水偏向多糖；乙醇偏向三萜及部分酚类。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** General wellness; antioxidant and immune-support preparations
  日常体质、抗氧化及免疫支持
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Kidney stone / high-oxalate risk; caution with blood-thinning or blood-sugar-lowering medication
  肾结石/高草酸风险；抗凝、降糖药物使用者慎用

## Birch Polypore 桦树多孔菌

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral 中性
- **Benefits 功效:** Traditionally used for digestive and general wellness; studied for antioxidant, antimicrobial and immune-related activity.
  传统用于消化及整体健康；研究关注抗氧化、抗菌及免疫相关作用。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** Polysaccharides, triterpenoids, phenolic compounds
  多糖、三萜、酚类
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water + ethanol. Hot water extracts polysaccharides; ethanol extracts the more hydrophobic compounds.
  热水＋乙醇。热水提取多糖；乙醇提取较疏水成分。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** Digestive and general wellness
  消化及整体健康支持
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Pregnancy/breastfeeding; mushroom allergy; multiple medications
  孕期、哺乳期、蘑菇过敏及多种药物使用者谨慎

## Red Belt Conk 红带菌

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral 中性
- **Benefits 功效:** Studied for antioxidant, antimicrobial and immune-related potential.
  研究方向主要包括抗氧化、抗菌及免疫相关活性。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** Polysaccharides, phenolics, triterpenoid-related compounds
  多糖、酚类、三萜相关成分
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water + ethanol. Suited to a broad-spectrum dual extraction.
  热水＋乙醇。适合较广谱的双相提取。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** General wellness; antioxidant and immune-support preparations
  整体健康、抗氧化及免疫支持
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Pregnancy/breastfeeding; mushroom allergy; concentrated extracts should not be self-used to treat disease
  孕哺期、蘑菇过敏者；不宜自行以浓缩提取物治疗疾病

## Artist Conk 画家灵芝

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral 中性
- **Benefits 功效:** Traditionally used as a medicinal polypore; *Ganoderma* research focuses on immune modulation, antioxidant and inflammation-related activity.
  传统作为药用多孔菌；灵芝属研究主要集中于免疫调节、抗氧化及炎症相关作用。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** Polysaccharides, triterpenoids, phenolic compounds
  多糖、三萜、酚类
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water + ethanol. Hot water favors polysaccharides; ethanol favors triterpenoids.
  热水＋乙醇。热水偏向多糖；乙醇偏向三萜等成分。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** General immune and wellness support
  日常免疫及整体体质支持
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Anticoagulant/antiplatelet medication; bleeding disorders; pregnancy/breastfeeding
  抗凝/抗血小板药物、出血风险、孕哺期者慎用

## Turkey Tail 云芝

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral 中性
- **Benefits 功效:** Well studied for immune modulation; its polysaccharide compounds are studied as adjuncts in cancer care.
  免疫调节研究较充分；其多糖类成分被研究用于癌症辅助治疗方向。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** β-glucans, PSK, PSP, polysaccharopeptides
  β-葡聚糖、PSK、PSP、多糖肽
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water. The main targets are polysaccharides, β-glucans and PSK/PSP; ethanol is not the main choice.
  热水。主要目标为多糖、β-葡聚糖、PSK/PSP。乙醇不是主要选择。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** Immune-support focused preparations
  免疫支持方向
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Should not replace cancer treatment; check with a professional first if on chemotherapy, immunotherapy or immunosuppressants
  不能替代癌症治疗；化疗、免疫治疗或使用免疫抑制剂者先咨询专业人士

## Reishi / Lingzhi 灵芝

- **Nature 性质:** Neutral / slightly warm 中性偏温
- **Benefits 功效:** Traditionally used to support qi, immune function, respiratory and general wellness; modern research focuses on immune modulation, antioxidant activity and inflammatory responses.
  传统用于扶正、补气及整体体质调理；现代研究主要关注免疫调节、抗氧化及炎症反应。
- **Main compounds 主要成分:** Polysaccharides, β-glucans, triterpenes (ganoderic acids), sterols, phenolic compounds
  多糖、β-葡聚糖、三萜（灵芝酸）、甾醇、酚类
- **Extraction 推荐提取:** Hot water + ethanol dual extraction. Hot water mainly extracts polysaccharides and β-glucans; ethanol is better for triterpenes, especially ganoderic acids.
  热水＋乙醇双提取。热水主要提取多糖、β-葡聚糖；乙醇更适合三萜类，尤其是灵芝酸。
- **Suitable 适合谁:** General wellness; immune and antioxidant support; traditional qi-tonifying preparations
  日常体质调理、免疫及抗氧化支持；传统扶正补气方向
- **Unsuitable 不适合谁:** Bleeding disorders; anticoagulant/antiplatelet medications; pregnancy/breastfeeding; before surgery
  出血性疾病、使用抗凝/抗血小板药物、孕哺期及手术前者需谨慎
